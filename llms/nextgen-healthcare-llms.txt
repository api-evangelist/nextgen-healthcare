# NextGen Healthcare

> NextGen Healthcare is a leading provider of healthcare technology solutions, serving over 10,000 medical groups and 100,000+ physicians. Our mission is to empower the transformation of ambulatory care with innovative tools, including AI-driven workflows, EHR optimization, and enhanced patient-provider experiences.

We are pioneering the integration of agentic AI, large language models (LLMs), and automation into real-world healthcare settings. Our commitment is to reduce administrative burden, improve care quality, and create a more human experience for clinicians and patients alike.
